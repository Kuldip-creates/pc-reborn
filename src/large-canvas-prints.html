Three finishes, one beautiful canvas

Choose how your photo continues around the sides. Each finish creates a different look while keeping the front image clear.

  • Gallery wrap

    Your photo continues around every edge for a seamless, borderless look that keeps the artwork feeling uninterrupted from every angle.

    • Image wraps around every side
    • No visible front border
    • A clean, contemporary finish

    Best for: Scenic photos and artwork

  • Mirror-effect wrap

    The outer pixels are reflected along the sides, preserving the complete front image while creating a neat, balanced edge.

    • Edge pixels are mirrored
    • Keeps the full photo in view
    • A balanced, consistent finish

    Best for: Photos with detail near the edges

  • Solid-colour wrap

    A solid-colour side gives your canvas a subtle framed look, helping faces, lettering and important details stay on the front.

    • Solid-colour side edges
    • Front image stays untouched
    • A clean, framed appearance

    Best for: Portraits and fine edge detail

Large-format tip: keep faces, lettering and important details away from the wrap area.

Large canvas print questions, answered

Guidance on choosing a size, preparing your photo, canvas wraps, hanging and delivery.

What size large canvas should I choose?

Measure your wall and use masking tape to preview the footprint. Large canvas prints are available up to 300 × 150 cm; custom dimensions can be selected in the design flow.

What resolution does my photo need?

Start with the clearest original image you have. The suitable resolution depends on the print size.

Which canvas wrap should I choose?

Gallery wrap continues your photo around every edge. Mirror effect reflects the outer pixels along the sides, keeping the full photo on the front. Solid colour gives plain side edges, so faces, lettering and fine detail stay on the front.

Does the canvas arrive ready to hang?

Every canvas comes with a free hanging kit: picture hook, screws and wall plugs.

How long do production and delivery take?

Canvas prints are usually dispatched in 1–2 days. Delivery time varies by service and destination; available services and charges are confirmed at checkout.

How should I care for my canvas?

Keep your canvas out of direct sunlight to help preserve its colours.
